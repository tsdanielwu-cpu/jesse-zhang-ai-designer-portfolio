# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, design leads, creative collaborators, and peers reviewing Zhang Zhaoxi's AI design practice on desktop. They need to understand the quality of the final work quickly, then inspect the thinking, tools, and process behind selected projects.

## Product Purpose

Present a focused AI designer portfolio in which the work is the primary content and the resume is supporting context. Success means visitors can identify the designer's visual point of view, understand five core projects, and confidently initiate contact.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, experimental, precise. The experience should feel like a confident creative studio or designer portfolio with a technical edge, while remaining readable and deliberate.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio templates with interchangeable cards and centered marketing sections.
- Decorative AI-purple gradients, excessive glass effects, or visual effects without narrative purpose.
- Oversized typography that obscures content hierarchy.
- PDF pages presented as the final web experience.
- Motion that hides content, bounces, tracks the pointer, or harms performance.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with real work and preserve image quality.
2. Translate process into native web storytelling instead of reproducing documents.
3. Use asymmetry and pacing to create distinction without sacrificing clarity.
4. Let motion reveal hierarchy and material, never gate access to content.
5. Keep contact and project navigation obvious within an expressive visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, semantic structure, keyboard-accessible links, descriptive image text, and a complete `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Avoid relying on color or animation alone to communicate meaning.
